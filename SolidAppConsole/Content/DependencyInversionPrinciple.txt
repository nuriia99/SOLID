D - DEPENDENCY INVERSION PRINCIPLE (SRP)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

📋 DEFINITION:
Depend on abstractions, not on concrete implementations.

💡 BENEFITS:
  • Low coupling.
  • Easy to test.
  • Flexible and extensible.

💡 DIP vs. Dependency Injection (DI):
Dependency injection is a design pattern. The class should not be responsible for creating
its dependencies. Instead, shifts the creation responsibility to another class.
