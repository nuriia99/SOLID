I - INTERFACE SEGREGATION PRINCIPLE (ISP)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

📋 DEFINITION:
Clients should not depend on interfaces they do not use.

💡 Concepts:
The idea is better to have multiple small interfaces than one large for general purposes.

💡 BENEFITS:
  • Cleaner interfaces. Specific interfaces are easier to interact with and test.
  • Less coupling. A change in one part won't breake another classes.
  • Greater flexibility. You can mix interfaces to create a class with complex behaviours.
